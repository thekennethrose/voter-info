Every vote comes from the Senate's official roll call record and links to it. A bill counts as a healthcare bill when the Congressional Research Service files it under Health, or when the vote's official title names healthcare, Medicare, Medicaid or the Affordable Care Act. Bills filed under another area that carry healthcare subject tags, like spending and defense bills, are listed separately.
